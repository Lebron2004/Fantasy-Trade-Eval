Every pick the Props page shows (the top picks and parlays for each pro sport, and the best spreads, totals and winners for college) is saved each morning and graded the next morning against the box score. A pick for a player who didn't play is a void, as at a book. Prop picks are made against a stand-in line (the line nearest the player's recent average), since free data has no real sportsbook lines, so the profit figures assume every pick was a 1-unit bet at -110. Model report cards come from games each model never saw while training. Past results don't promise future ones.
